Legendary Dragon

A cooperative game full of strategy and teamwork. Each turn, players debate, plan, and position the Bard, the Magician and the Shaman in front of the right monsters to maximize gains and try to win the round.

Roll the dice, activate your abilities at the right moment, team up to win the battle, and face creatures like Strong Goblins, Weak Skeletons or ... a Legendary Dragon!
